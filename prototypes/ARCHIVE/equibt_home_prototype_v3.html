The EQUIBT difference

Why most operational improvements do not hold. And what we do differently.

01
We start with the problem, not the solution.
Most engagements begin with a predetermined tool or methodology. We begin with a structured problem definition. FRAME aligns your leadership team on what is actually broken before any resource is committed. Solving the right problem is the highest-leverage thing we do.
02
We build capability inside your team, not dependency on ours.
Every EQUIBT engagement is designed to make itself unnecessary. We work alongside your people, transfer the analytical methods, install the management systems, and then step back deliberately. The test of our work is whether it holds after we leave.
03
We measure what matters, not what is easy to measure.
Most operational metrics are activity measures. We establish outcome measures: what the process is actually producing against what it should produce. The PLAN diagnostic builds the measurement foundation that every subsequent phase is grounded in. Evidence, not assumption.

28% → 9%

From 28% rework to 9% — and held for 18 months.

A large financial services operation had a persistent rework problem that three previous improvement programmes had failed to resolve. We used PLAN to establish the true baseline, SOLVE to identify the root cause (a combination of unclear decision criteria and inconsistent training), and built the measurement system to monitor it permanently.
