Why start here

Full commitment without validation is the highest-risk move in operations.

The pattern is common. A solution is designed. Leadership approves the budget. The full operation transitions to the new process. Three months in, unexpected problems surface - the new process does not handle edge cases, the team cannot sustain the new cadence, a dependency upstream was not accounted for. By then, the budget is spent, the old process is gone, and rollback is expensive.

A rapid pilot breaks this pattern. It is not a proof of concept. It is not a simulation. It runs real work through the new process with real people, real data, and real metrics. Typically one business unit, 4 to 6 weeks. Long enough to surface the problems that only appear under real operating conditions. Short enough to adjust before full commitment.

Once the pilot validates the direction, EVOLVE shifts into requirements development. Business requirements. Product requirements. Service design. Implementation planning. Change management planning. Everything the organisation needs to deploy at full scale with confidence.

What you receive

Four deliverables. Validated, then specified.

A4 document — Pilot Results Report

Pilot Results Report

A structured assessment of what the pilot revealed: what worked, what did not, what needs adjustment before full deployment. Metrics tracked against success criteria from FRAME. Go/no-go recommendation with supporting evidence. This is the evidence your leadership team needs to authorise full deployment.

A4 document — Business Requirements Document

Business Requirements Document

The complete specification for full-scale deployment. Business requirements, functional requirements, non-functional requirements. Structured so technology teams, operations teams, and training teams can all read from the same document. Written against validated pilot findings, not pre-pilot assumptions.

A4 document — Implementation and Change Management Plan

Implementation and Change Management Plan

A phased implementation plan with risk mitigation built in. Change management approach, communication plan, training needs analysis, and resistance management strategy. Designed to give the operation the best chance of adoption and sustainability.

A4 document — Deployment Authorisation Brief

Deployment Authorisation Brief

An executive summary of pilot results, go/no-go recommendation, and resource requirements for full deployment. Written for the leadership team, not the project team. Gives your board or investment committee what they need to authorise full rollout without reading the full pilot report.

Energy and Utilities EVOLVE

Problem

A field operations team had a designed process redesign that needed board sign-off for full deployment. Board required evidence the new process would work under real operating conditions before approving the budget.

Approach

Ran the new process in one regional depot for six weeks with live tracking against three KPIs from the original project charter. All three targets met by week four. Board approved full deployment at the post-pilot review. Requirements document developed during the pilot phase.
