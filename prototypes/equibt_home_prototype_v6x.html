OUR WORK

Proven in practice.

Problem, approach and outcome stay visible, without dressing anonymised work as a marketing claim.

Energy retailer
18% to 24%
retention save rate

Redesigned the customer retention process.

Problem

High churn across the residential customer base. Third-party call centres were operating without data integration or a consistent retention method, and each centre was running its own approach with little visibility of outcomes.

Approach

Mapped the end-to-end retention process across the call-centre partners, identified the failure points driving churn, and redesigned the process with the retention teams.

Outcome

The retention model now runs independently across partner teams without external support.

Utilities ombudsman
52 to 31 days
investigation cycle time

Standardised the investigation process across teams.

Problem

Five investigation teams were each running a different variant of the complaints process. A large backlog was building, resolution times varied by team, and the variation was creating compliance exposure.

Approach

Mapped the existing process across all five teams, identified variation points and root causes, and worked with investigators to redesign one standard process.

Outcome

All teams now operate from a single consistent investigation process with independent improvement capability.
