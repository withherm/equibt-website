WHY THIS SECTOR IS DIFFERENT

The same operational problem carries more consequence here.

A rework problem in a bank is shaped by regulatory documentation requirements, approval hierarchies, and the risk appetite of the compliance function. The same fix that works in a factory will not survive contact with an audit trail. We design improvement that holds inside the controls you already answer to.

Documentation burden

Audit and regulatory reporting can be carried by spreadsheets long after the operating risk is visible.

Approval hierarchy

Delegated authority and compliance review can turn one unclear handoff into repeat rework.

Risk appetite

The solution has to match the compliance function's tolerance for variation, not just the process owner's preference.

AI-assisted decisions

Credit, fraud and onboarding tools need ownership, measured benefit and drift detection.

High-volume handoffs

Handle times rise quickly when small ambiguity repeats across claims, lending and onboarding queues.

Customer impact

Back-office instability shows up as delays, complaints, remediation and avoidable escalation.

23% reduction

Reduced claims rework through a governed pilot.

Problem

A large financial services operation had a persistent claims rework problem that three previous improvement programmes had failed to fix. Each programme had treated the symptom: additional training, updated checklists, or system modifications. None had isolated where the work was actually breaking or which decision right was missing.

Approach

PLAN established the true baseline and mapped where rework was originating. SOLVE identified a missing decision right at a specific point in the approval process, combined with inconsistent training across teams. EVOLVE tested the structural fix in live work and GOVERN built the control infrastructure around the new decision point.

Outcomes

The operation stopped treating the symptom and locked the decision right into the process. The pilot created a clearer operating standard, gave leaders a better view of drift risk, and left the team with governance infrastructure for the improved process.
