Why the system matters
Most improvements hold for twelve months. Then the team turns over and the operation drifts back.

EQUIBT is built around one question: what would have to be true for this to still be working in three years? Every pillar in the system is designed around that answer, not around billable hours or dependency on our team.

Results that hold.

Financial Services
Reducing rework across a high-volume operations team

A large financial services operation was generating significant rework across its back-office processing team. Root cause analysis identified a combination of upstream handoff failures and an unclear accountability structure. SOLVE and GOVERN phases were deployed over eight months, redesigning the handoff process and establishing a monitoring system the team now runs independently.
