      WHY THIS MATTERS

      A clear problem is half the work.

      A well-framed problem is specific, measurable, and agreed. It says what is happening, where, how big it is, and why it matters, without naming a solution. Get that right and the rest of the work has somewhere to aim. Get it wrong and even good execution solves the wrong thing.
