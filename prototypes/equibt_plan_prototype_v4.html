Why start here

Most organisations act before they measure.

The pressure to act is real. Leadership wants solutions, not more analysis. But organisations that skip the diagnostic phase consistently solve the wrong problem, invest in the wrong interventions, and then wonder why improvements do not hold.

PLAN is the structured measurement phase that establishes what is actually happening, not what people think is happening. It produces a baseline, a capability assessment, and a performance gap analysis. These are the inputs that make everything after PLAN faster, cheaper, and more targeted.

Without PLAN, any improvement programme is guessing. With PLAN, every decision is grounded in evidence.

Why EQUIBT?

Evidence-based, not assumption-driven.

We do not accept anecdotal accounts of where the problem is. We measure it. Every PLAN engagement produces a quantified baseline that separates opinion from fact and gives your programme a defensible starting point.

Capability readiness, not process alone.

Process gaps and capability gaps are different problems with different solutions. PLAN assesses both. An organisation with strong processes but undertrained people will fail at implementation. We measure the full picture.

Roadmap, not recommendations.

PLAN does not produce a list of suggestions. It produces a prioritised roadmap with sequenced interventions, grounded in the measurement data. Your leadership team leaves knowing exactly what to address, in what order, and why.

What we examine

Three areas. Full picture.

Process Health

We map your core operational processes against their designed intent, measure actual cycle times and error rates, and identify where variance, rework, and waste are concentrated. The output is a quantified picture of where the process is and is not performing.

Process Health illustration

Capability Readiness

We assess the skills, knowledge, and behaviours your team needs versus what they currently demonstrate. This covers both technical capability (can they execute the process?) and leadership capability (can they sustain and improve it?). Gaps are quantified and prioritised.

Capability Readiness illustration

Measurement System

We audit your existing KPIs and data infrastructure: what you measure, how you measure it, whether the measures reflect performance or just activity, and whether they are visible at the right levels. A broken measurement system makes every other improvement invisible.

The work

Recent engagements.

Commercial Operations PLAN

Problem

A national financial services group had no consistent plan/do/review methodology across its state-based sales functions. Each state team ran their own reporting approach and review cadence. Senior leadership was fielding ad-hoc reporting requests weekly because no standard output existed. The business had grown rapidly and the planning infrastructure had not kept pace.

Approach

Mapped the end-to-end commercial planning process across all state teams. Identified where reporting diverged and where decisions were being made without consistent data. Worked with the commercial leadership team to design standardised forums, decision rights, and a single reporting pack. Piloted the new cadence with two state teams before national rollout. The team sustained the approach for two years without external support.

Outcomes

60%

Commercial planning standardised nationally. Ad-hoc reporting requests eliminated as a recurring drain on leadership time.

Government and Public Sector PLAN

Problem

1,770 enquiries per month. 183-category taxonomy driving inconsistency. More than a third of inbound calls misdirected or out-of-jurisdiction, all handled manually. No agreed KPIs. Informal quality assurance.

Approach

Analysed enquiry volume across the full 183-category taxonomy to identify true call types versus classification noise. Ran structured root cause analysis on the out-of-jurisdiction volume to understand why it was arriving and being handled manually. Delivered a 12-recommendation roadmap including an IVR automation specification, simplified 30-category taxonomy, a quality assurance framework, and a revised workforce model.

Outcomes

32%

Category taxonomy reduced from 183 to 30. High-volume inbound calls identified and specified for automation.
