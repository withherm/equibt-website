WHO WE ARE

We make operational improvement hold.

Most improvement programmes produce results. Fewer produce results that last. The common failure mode is not bad methodology, it is abandonment: a firm runs a diagnostic, designs the fix, runs a pilot, then leaves, and twelve months later the operation has drifted back.

We built EQUIBT around a different model. Every engagement is scoped from the outset to end with your team running the improved operation independently, without us in the room. We embed inside your operations, build capability in your people, and exit when the work is yours. Operating since 2009.

HOW WE WORK

Three things make the improvement survive our exit.

Sustainment, not just delivery.

You get an exit plan from day one, with the exit criteria agreed before the work starts. We do not move to the next phase until the current one is embedded in how your team operates.

Capability at the centre.

You leave able to run the improved operation yourselves. We measure success by what your team can do once we go, not by what we delivered while we were there.

Governance that runs itself.

You keep the controls that keep the gain. Process architecture, control plans, dashboards, and escalation protocols are the mechanism that holds the improvement in place.
