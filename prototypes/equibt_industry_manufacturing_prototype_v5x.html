WHY THIS SECTOR IS DIFFERENT

The floor has to run on the hard days too.

A process problem on a manufacturing floor is shaped by shift patterns, machine uptime, and supplier variability. Improvement has to work across shifts and survive a bad batch of inputs. We design for the floor as it runs, not the line on a good day.

Cycle time and throughput below capacity

Capacity leaks through waiting, variation and handoffs that become normal over time.

Quality variation and rework at the line

Small deviations at the point of work become rework, scrap and missed delivery windows.

Changeover and downtime losses

Lost time around changeover and uptime needs to be measured before it can be improved.

Supplier variability destabilising the process

The operating standard has to survive imperfect inputs, not only ideal conditions.

Improvement capability that does not outlast the consultant

The floor needs the capability to keep improving after the external project closes.

AI and automation deployed without floor-level governance

Automation needs standards and ownership at the point where work actually changes.

OUR WORK

Proven in practice.

Precision manufacturer
PLAN | Measure SOLVE | Analyse
14% reduction

Reduced cycle time across ANZ operations.

Problem

A precision manufacturer had cycle time and throughput below capacity across ANZ operations. Shift patterns, machine uptime and supplier variability were all affecting the process, which made the problem hard to isolate. The operation needed a fix that worked across shifts, not only on a good day.

Approach

PLAN established the baseline and mapped how the work actually ran across shifts. SOLVE isolated the structural causes of waste, rework and delay. The future state was designed around the operating constraints of the floor, including uptime and supplier variability.

Outcomes

The line gained a clearer standard for where time was being lost and how the process should run. Leaders had better evidence for prioritising improvements, and the floor had a practical basis for continued improvement without consultant dependency.
