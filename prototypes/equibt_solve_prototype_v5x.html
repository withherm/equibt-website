WHY THIS MATTERS

Treating symptoms is why improvements do not hold.

Causes, not symptoms. Structured analysis isolates why the problem exists, not just where it shows up.

The most common failure mode in operational improvement is fixing symptoms rather than causes. A process is slow, so automation gets added. The automation speeds up the wrong thing. A team has high rework, so training gets deployed. The rework continues because the root cause is a missing decision right upstream, not a skill gap.

SOLVE prevents this. It applies structured analytical methods - fishbone diagrams, five-why analysis, value stream mapping, statistical process control - to isolate what is actually causing the problem. Then it designs the future state: what the process should look like, what roles and decision rights need to change, what technology enablement is needed, and what it will cost.

Where fundamental redesign is needed - not incremental improvement but structural change to how work flows - SOLVE includes operational reengineering. New process flows. New decision rights. New accountability structures. Designed with your team, costed with clear trade-offs, ready for pilot validation in EVOLVE.

OUR WORK

Proven in practice.

Financial Services
SOLVE | Analyse
47% reduction

Redesigned claims exception routing.

Problem

High error rate in a claims processing function. Training had been deployed twice with no improvement. The problem was attributed to skill gaps.

Approach

Root cause analysis identified that the decision gate for exception routing was missing - claims were being processed by whoever picked them up, not by the team qualified to handle exceptions. Process redesign added the decision gate.

Outcomes

Processing errors eliminated within four weeks of redesign.

Redesigned high-volume regulatory processes.

Problem

A regulatory agency needed to redesign its highest-volume processes after a diagnostic identified systemic rework. Previous improvement attempts had failed because root cause analysis was skipped.

Approach

The redesigned processes held after handover, with the internal team able to operate and improve them without external support.
