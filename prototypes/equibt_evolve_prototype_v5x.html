Validate the future state before you commit the full operation.

A rapid pilot tests the designed solution with real data in a controlled scope. Once validated, we develop the requirements for full-scale deployment. Decision-support, then build-readiness.

Michael is close to a significant operational change. The future state is designed. The cost-benefit looks promising on paper. But committing the entire operation to change based on analysis alone carries risk. What if the process works in theory but fails in practice? What if the team cannot adopt the new way of working at the pace required? What if there are dependencies nobody saw until real work hit the new process?

A rapid pilot removes this uncertainty. It runs the redesigned process in a controlled scope - one business unit, real customers, live metrics. You see what works, what breaks, and what needs adjustment before the full operation commits. And once the pilot validates the direction, EVOLVE shifts to requirements development: specifying exactly what needs to be built, trained, and deployed for the solution to work at full scale.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Full commitment without validation is the highest-risk move in operations.

Validate first. Run the redesigned process under real operating conditions before the full organisation commits.

The pattern is common. A solution is designed. Leadership approves the budget. The full operation transitions to the new process. Three months in, unexpected problems surface - the new process does not handle edge cases, the team cannot sustain the new cadence, a dependency upstream was not accounted for. By then, the budget is spent, the old process is gone, and rollback is expensive.

A rapid pilot breaks this pattern. It is not a proof of concept. It is not a simulation. It runs real work through the new process with real people, real data, and real metrics. Typically one business unit, 4 to 6 weeks. Long enough to surface the problems that only appear under real operating conditions. Short enough to adjust before full commitment.

Once the pilot validates the direction, EVOLVE shifts into requirements development. Business requirements. Product requirements. Service design. Implementation planning. Risk mitigation strategies. Change management planning. Everything the organisation needs to deploy at full scale with confidence.

Real data, real metrics.

Not a simulation. Not a desktop exercise. Real work, real customers, real measurement. You see what actually happens.

Controlled scope, controlled risk.

One business unit. 4 to 6 weeks. If something breaks, the blast radius is contained.

Requirements after validation, not before.

We specify what needs to be built after the pilot proves the concept, not before. This prevents requirements being written against unvalidated assumptions.

Problem

A field operations team had a designed process redesign that needed board sign-off for full deployment. The board required evidence that the new process would work under real operating conditions before approving the budget.
